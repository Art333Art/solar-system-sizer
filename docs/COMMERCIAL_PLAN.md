# 30-day revenue experiment

Target: at least **£20 received**, not merely clicks or unqualified leads. Safety results must remain independent of commercial placement.

## Recommended first offer

Maintain a short, contextual equipment shortlist after verifying every destination and material claim. Use the approved [Amazon UK Associates programme](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/) links only with the required disclosure; never recommend protection equipment or core PV hardware solely for commission.

In parallel, approach 10 UK MCS installers and two EV-charger vendors with a simple paid-lead pilot. Andersen publicly operates an affiliate programme for qualifying electrical professionals, but eligibility and terms must be confirmed before it is mentioned in-product. A single £20+ paid, consented installer lead is a more plausible first-month target than relying only on low-value retail commissions.

## Weekly execution

- Days 1–3: deploy on an owned domain, replace the placeholder commercial section with a real inbox/form, add privacy/cookie/affiliate disclosures, and instrument anonymous funnel events.
- Days 4–7: publish three evidence-led pages (string compatibility, battery sizing, EV solar charging) and apply to appropriate programmes using the live site URL.
- Week 2: contact 10 installers with a capped pilot; qualify geography, MCS status, response time, lead price and consent language in writing.
- Week 3: add only approved, labelled placements; use stable redirect IDs so destinations can change without touching calculations.
- Week 4: report visits → completed calculations → outbound clicks → consented leads → accepted leads → cash received. Stop placements with complaints or no buyer value.

## Definition of done

At least £20 has cleared, the revenue source is recorded, every paid relationship is disclosed beside its link, and no safety recommendation is ranked by commission.
